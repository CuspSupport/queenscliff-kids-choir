What Happens?

One friendly choir, all grades welcome — held right on school grounds.

Tuesday Mornings

All Grades — 1 to 6

Meet at the Grade 5/6 portable classroom at 8:00am.

We warm up our voices, then sing and listen together, collaborating on songs and harmonies. We finish by 8:45am, in plenty of time to get to class. For those who want to, we work towards presenting a song at school assembly.
